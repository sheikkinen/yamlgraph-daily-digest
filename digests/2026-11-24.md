## [A training engine for your local model, using your idle compute](https://github.com/sous-labs/songe)

Songe is an open-source tool that lets a local model used through opencode, pi, or any OpenAI-compatible client learn from the user's workflow. It observes tool results, errors, and follow-up messages, then fine-tunes the model during idle periods and deploys only if it passes checks. All data stays on the local machine.

## [The KL Divergence Is Never Negative, Except in PyTorch](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.30835)

The paper finds widespread value and gradient errors in PyTorch's KL divergence implementations across 18 closed-form families and F.kl_div, with over 85% of FP32 scalar results showing at least 1% relative error near KL 10^(-7). It shows higher precision alone does not fix the problem, while stable closed-form decompositions, compensated arithmetic, and analytic gradients remove over 97% of significant errors.

*Why: Strong unique insight for Python and AI practitioners, since PyTorch's core loss-related math is shown to be unreliable with concrete fixes.*
