## [Stricter checks for uploaded distributions on PyPI](https://blog.pypi.org/posts/2026-11-18-stricter-checks-for-uploaded-sdists/)

PyPI has added checks that can reject uploaded source distributions and wheels to make Python packaging more resistant to parser differentials. Further checks are planned to make PyPI's handling stricter and more conformant to packaging specifications.
