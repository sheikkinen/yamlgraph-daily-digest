## [Frostbite: A new CPU architecture discovered, designed and verified by AI](https://architectlabs.com/blog/frostbite)

Architect Labs describes Frostbite, a general-purpose CPU with a new instruction set that its AI systems discovered, designed, and verified end-to-end. It reportedly achieved a 4.59x performance-per-watt gain over a silicon-proven dual-issue in-order superscalar core across 25 workloads, 15 of which were held out until final evaluation.

*Why: A notable example of AI doing end-to-end hardware design, though the source is a company announcement and the claims need verification.*

## [Signal Is Serious About Protecting Your Privacy](https://signal.org/blog/protecting-your-privacy/)

*Why: Timely and consequential AI-platform story about the tension between OS-level AI agents and user privacy.*

## [Rebuilding my local voice assistant pipeline](https://blog.platypush.tech/article/Rewriting-my-voice-assistant-pipeline)

Fabio Manganiello rewrites his self-hosted voice assistant in Platypush, replacing openWakeWord with microWakeWord for wake word detection and adopting OpenAI-based speech-to-text, responses, and text-to-speech. He was dissatisfied with false positives, false negatives, and weak speech boundary detection in noisy environments with cheap microphones.

*Why: A hands-on, Python-adjacent build combining AI speech services with a self-hosted pipeline, offering practical engineering lessons.*
