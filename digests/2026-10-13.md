## [Janas: Run LLMs larger than RAM by streaming experts from SSD](https://github.com/prabanta-dev/janas)

Janas is a C-based LLM inference engine that enables running large language models larger than available RAM by streaming model weights from SSD storage. It demonstrates significant performance improvements over llama.cpp, achieving 37 tok/s with Qwen3-Next-80B on a 32GB laptop compared to 2.8 tok/s with llama.cpp, with support for both CPU and GPU inference.

*Why: Critical technical innovation for AI practitioners. Enables running large models on consumer hardware with dramatic performance improvements, directly relevant to AI accessibility.*

## [An hour of audio transcribed in about 13s on MacBook Air](https://eg2.haku.sh/)

Google's EmbeddingGemma 2, a multimodal embedding model, was adapted into a speech-to-text system that transcribes an hour of audio in approximately 13 seconds entirely on-device on a MacBook Air. The approach uses the model's existing audio encoder and adds a small trained reader that maps audio slices to the nearest words in a shared embedding space, with a secondary model handling punctuation.

*Why: Breaking technical achievement demonstrating significant AI performance improvements on consumer hardware. Showcases innovative application of existing models.*

## [Apple's Minions: A virtual GPU cloud built from idle Apple Silicon Macs](https://machinelearning.apple.com/research/distributed-gpu-cloud)

Apple researchers present Minions, a distributed virtual GPU cloud that aggregates idle Apple Silicon GPUs across macOS devices to execute machine learning inference workloads. The system addresses limitations of centralized GPU infrastructure by providing flexible, fault-tolerant resource utilization without requiring additional hardware procurement.

*Why: Significant infrastructure innovation for AI inference. Demonstrates novel approach to distributed GPU computing with practical implications for ML deployment.*
